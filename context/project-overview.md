# ZhenZhen

## Overview

ZhenZhen is a post-lesson Chinese practice web app for one teacher/admin
and her invited students. The teacher can publish class documents and assign short,
engaging practice activities after a lesson. Students complete the
activities, submit their work to the teacher, maintain a daily fire
streak, stay motivated through friendly rankings, and exchange
voice-only messages in a real-time Voice Conversation tab.

## Goals

1. Let a teacher publish lesson resources and assign practice quickly
2. Give students varied Chinese practice with clear, focused activities
3. Encourage consistent daily practice through streaks and friendly competition

## Core User Flow

1. The teacher/admin signs in and invites a student's Gmail address
2. The student accepts the email invitation and signs in with the matching account
3. The teacher publishes lesson documents and creates an assignment
4. The student opens the assigned post-lesson practice
5. The student studies flashcards and completes the required activities
6. The student submits answers, writing, or voice recordings
7. The teacher reviews the submission and provides feedback
8. Eligible completed practice updates the student's streak and ranking
9. Active members can exchange voice messages in Voice Conversation

## Features

### Teaching and Class Content

- Teacher-managed classes, students, lessons, and post-class documents
- Gmail invitations, pending-invite management, and student removal
- Assignments composed from one or more supported practice activities
- A teacher view of student submissions and completion status

### Student Practice

- Quick flashcards for vocabulary and lesson review
- Multiple-choice questions with A, B, C, and D answers
- Chinese character, word, sentence, or short-response writing
- Voice recording for pronunciation and accent practice
- A chat-style Voice Conversation tab that sends voice messages only
- Real-time arrival of new voice messages with replayable history
- Submission history and teacher feedback

### Motivation and Identity

- Daily fire streak for consistent practice
- Points and a friendly class ranking/leaderboard
- Positive progress feedback that encourages the next practice session
- A fixed selection of five or six approved avatars

## Scope

### In Scope

- Responsive web experiences for teachers and students
- Email and Google sign-in with invite-only student access
- Lesson resources, practice creation, completion, submission, and review
- Flashcard, multiple-choice, writing, and voice-recording activities
- Voice-only conversation with real-time message delivery
- Restricted avatars, daily streaks, points, and class rankings

### Out of Scope

- Live video classes or real-time classroom teaching
- Text messages or live voice calls inside Voice Conversation
- User-uploaded custom avatars
- A native iOS or Android application in the initial version
- Unspecified automatic accent scoring until evaluation rules are defined

## Success Criteria

1. A teacher can publish a lesson resource and assign post-class practice
2. An enrolled student can complete and submit every supported activity type
3. The teacher can review each authorized student's submission
4. An eligible completion updates the correct streak and class ranking
5. Students cannot access another student's private submission
6. The teacher can invite a Gmail address, revoke a pending invitation,
   or remove an active student
7. An authorized member can send a voice message and other authorized
   members receive it without refreshing the conversation
